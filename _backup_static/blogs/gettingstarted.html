Overview

Invoice Hub is designed to simplify how businesses manage estimates, invoices, vendor bills, payments, and financial reporting. By centralizing these processes into a single platform, businesses can streamline financial workflows while maintaining organized and accurate records.

This guide provides an introduction to how Invoice Hub works and how businesses can begin managing their financial operations efficiently.

What You Can Manage

InvoiceHub allows businesses to organize key financial operations in one system, including:

  • Customer and vendor profiles
  • Estimates and approval workflows
  • Invoice creation and document attachments
  • Payment tracking and outstanding balances
  • Vendor bills and expense management
  • Automated invoice data capture
  • Tax configuration and automated tax calculation
  • Financial reports and operational insights

These capabilities help businesses maintain consistent billing processes and financial transparency.

How It Works in Invoice Hub

  • Invoice Hub connects multiple financial workflows through a structured process.
  • Customer and product records are first created within the system. These records are then used when generating estimates and invoices. Once an estimate is approved, it can be converted directly into an invoice for billing.
  • Businesses can record payments against invoices while the system automatically updates outstanding balances.
  • Vendor profiles allow businesses to upload vendor bills and track payables, while reports provide insights into receivables, expenses, and financial activity.
  • This integrated workflow allows businesses to manage both incoming and outgoing financial transactions from one platform.

Operational Impact

Using Invoice Hub helps businesses:

  • Maintain organized financial records
  • Reduce manual billing tasks
  • Improve invoice and payment tracking
  • Monitor receivables and payables efficiently
  • Gain better visibility into financial performance

By consolidating billing, expenses, and reporting into a single system, Invoice Hub enables businesses to manage financial operations with greater accuracy and control.
